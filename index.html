Halde® brings material and form together, turning raw wood, steel and light into objects that make a room feel like home

At Halde®, every piece starts with a sketch and ends with a hand. We work as a small studio of designers and craftspeople, shaping each chair, table and lamp with intention and care. Joints are tested, curves are sanded twice, and nothing is added without reason.

We choose honest materials — FSC oak, recycled aluminium, hand-blown glass, natural linen — and let them speak. We produce in small batches, close to home, so every object can be repaired, not replaced.

The Process

01/04

Sketch

Every piece begins on paper. We study how a body sits, how a hand reaches, how light falls — and draw until the form feels inevitable.

Prototype

Full-scale mock-ups in foam, plywood and steel. We sit, lean, lift and live with them for weeks before a single line is final.

Craft

Small-batch production with workshops within 200 km of Copenhagen. Solid joints, natural oils and finishes that age beautifully.

Live & Beyond

Delivered in plastic-free packaging, with a 10-year guarantee and spare parts for life. We repair, refinish and take pieces back.

Bespoke

Need something made for your space? Every package includes design consultation, material samples and white-glove delivery.

Let's Talk!

Still curious? Ask us anything about materials, delivery or custom work.

Ask a question →
How long does delivery take?

A In-stock pieces ship within 5 working days across Europe. Made-to-order pieces take 6–10 weeks, and we keep you updated at every stage.

Which materials do you use?

A FSC-certified oak, ash and walnut; recycled aluminium and steel; natural linen, wool and leather from tanneries we visit ourselves.

Can I see pieces before buying?

A Yes — visit our Copenhagen showroom, or order free material samples sent to your door within the EU.

Do you offer a warranty?

A Every Halde® piece comes with a 10-year structural guarantee and lifetime access to spare parts and refinishing.

Do you work with architects and hotels?

A Absolutely. Our Trade programme offers 20% off, CAD files, custom finishes and a dedicated project manager.
